Brento is ready

It is open in your browser now and walks you through the rest.

To open it later: press Cmd and Space, type Brento and press Return.

macOS may report a new background item called Brento. That is Brento starting when you log in. You can switch it off in System Settings, General, Login Items.

To remove Brento: open Uninstall Brento the same way. Your notes, your board and your calendar stay as they are.
