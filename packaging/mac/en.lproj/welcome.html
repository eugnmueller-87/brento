Welcome to Brento

One screen that tells you what needs you today: your tasks, your board and your meetings.

  1. Click Continue, then Install. It takes about a minute.
  2. Brento opens in your browser and asks one question per page. You can skip every one of them.

Brento brings everything it needs and installs only for you, so there is no admin password. It runs on this Mac, keeps its settings here and only reads: it never changes a note, a card or an appointment. Notes go to Claude and messages to Slack only if you switch that on.

If an administrator is asked for all the same, click Change Install Location and choose Install for me only.
