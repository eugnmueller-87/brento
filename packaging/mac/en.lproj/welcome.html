Welcome to Brento

One screen that tells you what needs you today: your tasks, your board and your meetings.

  1. Click Continue, then Install. It takes about a minute.
  2. Brento opens in your browser and asks one question per page. You can skip every one of them.

Brento brings everything it needs and installs only for you, so there is no admin password. Everything stays on this Mac, and Brento only reads: it never changes a note, a card or an appointment.
